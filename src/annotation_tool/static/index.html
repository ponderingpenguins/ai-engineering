Keys: i = first lying frame, o = last lying frame, x = toggle this frame, Delete = remove the interval here, r = mark the recording reviewed (or not), n = next recording that is not reviewed, Ctrl/Cmd+Z = undo, Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z = redo, space = play/pause, left/right = one frame (shift = ten), up/down = recording. Click the strip to jump. Positions are counted from 0 in capture order. The strip shows only your marks. Every change is saved at once.

Lying means the torso is on the floor and roughly horizontal (face up, face down or on the side). The first lying frame of a fall is the first one with the torso at floor level, and the last is the last one before the torso starts to lift. Sitting, kneeling, crawling and leaning on the hands are not lying. When unsure, do not mark.
